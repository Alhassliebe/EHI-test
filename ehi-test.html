Edinburgh Handedness Inventory (EHI)

A 20-item self-report questionnaire that measures hand preference for everyday tasks and yields a Laterality Quotient (LAQ) from −100 (extreme left-handedness) to +100 (extreme right-handedness).

Based on: Oldfield, R.C. (1971). The assessment and analysis of handedness: The Edinburgh inventory. Neuropsychologia, 9(1), 97-113.
For each activity below, indicate your hand preference. If you would never perform the activity, leave it on "No preference." If your preference is so strong you would never use the other hand, choose "Always." If you use either hand equally often, choose "No preference."
Please answer all 20 items before calculating your result.
